Hotel Suggestions

I basically chose the nicest hotels nearby me I could find - most of them in the nearby Tamarindos neighborhood which is very popular for visitors and is a few minutes away from me by vehicle. Tamarindos is great because it is close to everything but also quiet and you won't hear the nightlife this town is famous for. Puerto isn't really an upscale destination, but these days we have some pretty great options to stay at.
